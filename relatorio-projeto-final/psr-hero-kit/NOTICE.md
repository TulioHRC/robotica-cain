# Third-party material

- `sim/assets/hero/` (`base_link.obj`, `wheel.obj` and their `.mtl`): HeRo 3D
  models from [verlab/hero_common](https://github.com/verlab/hero_common)
  (`hero_description`), distributed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. They are used for
  non-commercial teaching only.
- [Genesis](https://github.com/Genesis-Embodied-AI/genesis-world) (Apache-2.0),
  PyTorch (BSD-3) and ROS Noetic are installed from their official sources when
  the images are built; they are not part of this repository.
